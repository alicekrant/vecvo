01 — Aging Biology

Hallmarks & Interventions

Cellular and molecular basis of biological aging: genomic instability, epigenetic drift, telomere attrition, proteostasis failure, deregulated nutrient sensing, mitochondrial dysfunction, cellular senescence, stem cell exhaustion, and altered intercellular communication.

02 — Oncogenesis

Cancer as Aging Disease

Cancer incidence increases exponentially with age. Age-associated DNA damage accumulation, immune senescence reducing immunosurveillance, pro-inflammatory SASP creating permissive tumor microenvironments, and epigenetic reprogramming all converge to drive oncogenesis.

12
Interventions catalogued
03 — Neurodegeneration

Alzheimer, Parkinson & Beyond

Neurodegenerative diseases are unified by age-dependent proteostasis collapse, mitochondrial failure, neuroinflammation mediated by activated microglia, and loss of synaptic integrity.

14
Interventions catalogued
04 — Psychiatric Disease

Inflammaging & the Brain

Major depression, bipolar disorder, and schizophrenia are increasingly framed as diseases of accelerated biological aging. Inflammatory cytokines, oxidative stress, HPA axis dysregulation, and microbiome disruption link systemic aging biology to psychiatric phenotypes.

Neuroplasticity & Performance

Evidence-based interventions for enhancing cognitive function in healthy individuals: sleep architecture optimization, exercise-induced BDNF release, dietary patterns, ketone metabolism, pharmacological and nutraceutical nootropics, and targeted neuromodulation.

SIRT7 Mini-Review: Role in Aging and Epigenetic Regulation

2026Mini-ReviewZenodo · CC BY 4.0

Sirtuin-7 functions as a histone deacylase with roles in ribosomal RNA transcription, DNA damage response, and chromatin remodeling. This review covers SIRT7 enzymatic activity, substrate specificity, metabolic regulation, and its emerging role as a longevity-relevant target.

2025Literature ReviewOSF Preprints · CC BY 4.0

Systematic documentation of the twelve-hallmarks framework (López-Otín et al. 2023) with mechanistic detail, evidence hierarchy across model organisms, human translational data, and current therapeutic approaches for each hallmark. 280+ primary citations.

Extended analysis of NAD+ biosynthetic pathways, age-dependent decline mechanisms, sirtuin and PARP competition, CD38 upregulation, and the precursor supplementation literature (NR vs. NMN). Includes pharmacokinetics, bioavailability data, and clinical trial status.

08 — Project

About This Compilation

Scope

This database covers five interconnected domains: biological aging, oncogenesis, neurodegeneration, psychiatric disease, and cognitive optimization. The organizing thesis is that aging is the primary substrate for all other listed pathologies.

Evidence Standards

Evidence is graded by study type: High (RCTs / large prospective cohorts, n>1000), Moderate (observational human data or replicated mammalian studies), Low (animal model data without human validation), Preliminary (in vitro or single early-stage studies).

Methodology

Primary literature searched via PubMed, bioRxiv, and Google Scholar. No commercial affiliation. No supplement industry funding. Interpretations represent current scientific consensus where it exists; contested claims are flagged explicitly.

Independence

Vecvo Research Initiative is an independent project. No institutional affiliation, no pharmaceutical funding, no commercial interests. All content is CC BY 4.0 — free to use with attribution.
